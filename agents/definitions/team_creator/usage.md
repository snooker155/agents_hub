Use this agent to design and maintain agent teams (bounded rosters that work a goal together).

Good fits:
- "Put together a team that can take a feature from spec to reviewed code"
- "Add a security reviewer to the platform team and say what it owns"
- "Switch this team to autonomous mode and make the BA the entry point"
- "Cut this team's rounds to 4 and cap it at $1.50"

Also good fits (the run tools refuse until you approve the cost):
- "Run it on this spec and give me the answer"
- "How did the last run end?"
- "Stop it"

Poor fits:
- Reading the round-by-round board — that is the team page
- Creating the agents to staff it — that is the Agent Creator's job

How to invoke:
- Describe the work the team has to do; the agent picks the mode, staffs the roster and writes each member's manifest
- For changes, name the team (or its id) and the exact edit
- The agent reports the resulting team_id and the terms on which the team stops talking
