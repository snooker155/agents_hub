Use this agent to build and maintain the worlds playground scenarios are cast in.

Good fits:
- "Build a world: a harbour customs house at night, guards who may search the crew, and a crate that has to reach the yard before the alarm hits five"
- "Start from the inn template but make it a monastery, and give the abbot the only key"
- "Add a cellar under the tavern that only connects to the kitchen"
- "Give every character a purse, and an action that pays somebody from it"
- "Only the harbourmaster should be able to open the gate"
- "Why does this world say my action changes a value it does not have?"

Poor fits:
- Casting agents, setting tick caps or starting a run — that is the Scenario Creator and the scenario page
- Anything outside the playground

How to invoke:
- Describe the place and, more importantly, what there is to fight over in it
- For changes, name the world (or its id) and the exact edit
- The agent reports the world_id and env_id, and anything still left to fix
