Campaign: The Sunken Vault of Maro-Tel
Session 7 — Dungeon Master's notes
Party: Kaelen (human warlock), Brynna (dwarf artificer), Oski (halfling rogue)

=== ITEMS RECOVERED ===

The Emberglass Wand — a slender wand of fused volcanic glass, warm to the touch.
It belongs to Kaelen now; he pried it from the vault guardian's hand. The wand can
channel the Cinder Lance spell three times per day without spending a spell slot,
but every third casting cracks the glass a little further. Brynna estimates it will
shatter after roughly twenty more castings unless repaired with mooncopper.

The Tidebound Amulet — a silver disc engraved with a spiral wave, worn by Oski.
The amulet protects its wearer from the Drowning Whisper curse that permeates the
lower vault. It does nothing against ordinary drowning, which Oski learned the
hard way in the flooded antechamber.

The Lantern of Patient Echoes — an iron lantern that burns without fuel. When lit,
it reveals the invisible script on vault walls, which is the only way to read the
Warden Glyphs. The lantern dims whenever the Hollow Choir spell is sung nearby;
the two cannot be used together.

Mooncopper Ingot — a fist-sized bar of pale metal recovered from the forge room.
Brynna carries it. Mooncopper is the only material that can repair the Emberglass
Wand, and it is also a required component for casting Stitch the Broken.

=== SPELLS LEARNED OR ENCOUNTERED ===

Cinder Lance — a bolt of compressed flame. Kaelen learned it from the vault
guardian's spellbook. It is the party's main answer to the ice-wraiths on level
three, who are immune to ordinary weapons but burn readily.

Drowning Whisper — the curse laid on the lower vault by the priests of Maro-Tel.
Anyone who hears the whisper begins to inhale water instead of air. The Tidebound
Amulet blocks it; the spell Stillsong suppresses it for one hour in a small area.

Stillsong — a quiet counter-hymn Brynna transcribed from the Warden Glyphs. It
suppresses the Drowning Whisper and calms hostile water elementals, but singing it
exhausts the caster: after each use, Brynna takes a level of fatigue.

Hollow Choir — a forbidden chant inscribed on the sealed door of the inner vault.
The party has NOT learned it. Reading the Warden Glyphs revealed that the Hollow
Choir opens the inner vault but extinguishes every light source in the dungeon,
including the Lantern of Patient Echoes, for a full day.

Stitch the Broken — a mending ritual Brynna knows. It can repair magical items,
but each repair consumes one mooncopper ingot. The party has exactly one ingot,
so they must choose: repair the Emberglass Wand now, or keep the ingot in case
the Tidebound Amulet is damaged in the inner vault.

=== SESSION EVENTS WORTH REMEMBERING ===

- The party decided NOT to learn the Hollow Choir yet. Losing the lantern for a
  day was judged too risky while ice-wraiths still roam level three. They will
  return after clearing the level.
- Oski nearly drowned in the flooded antechamber because the party assumed the
  Tidebound Amulet protected against all water. It only blocks the curse. Marked
  as a hard lesson: read item descriptions aloud before relying on them.
- Brynna successfully cast Stillsong to escort the party past the whisper zone,
  taking one level of fatigue. She can attempt it again tomorrow.
