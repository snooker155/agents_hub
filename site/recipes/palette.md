# Palette

Every color in the interface comes from a palette: a brand color, optional accent colors for success and danger, and a neutral tint. Customize it for yourself, for your entire workspace, or use one of four presets.

## What you get

A custom color theme applied instantly across the entire interface. Change your personal colors without affecting teammates. Set a workspace default that applies to everyone in that workspace. Light and dark modes both adapt automatically.

## Before you start

- A brand color as a hex code, for example `#003366`
- Account access to save your personal preference

## Steps

1. Go to **Account** and open the **Palette** section.

2. Pick a preset or start with Custom:
   - Navy (the default)
   - Forest
   - Slate
   - Sunset
   - Custom

3. Edit the brand color by pasting a hex code. The whole palette regenerates automatically.

4. Optionally set accent colors:
   - Neutral tint (defaults to gray if left empty)
   - Success accent (green if empty)
   - Danger accent (red if empty)

5. Watch the preview: all 11 shades per color are computed in OKLCH color space, keeping lightness fixed so contrast is safe in both light and dark modes.

6. Check the contrast warning (if any): it shows whether your brand color meets WCAG AA at 4.5:1. The warning is informational. Saving is always allowed because the computed ramp is safe regardless.

7. Click **Save**. A signed in person's preference is saved to their account; without an account it stays in this browser.

8. To set a workspace default, open the workspace's page and, under its **Agents** tab, set **Default palette**.

9. Everyone in that workspace sees that default when they switch to it (unless they have set a personal override).

10. Click **Reset** to remove your personal choice and fall back to the workspace default or built-in.
